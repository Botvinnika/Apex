Our mission

The African sports market deserves better data.

The sports analytics industry treats Africa as an afterthought. The big models cover the Premier League, La Liga, NBA — and stop there. Local leagues get vague gut-feel "predictions" from Telegram tipsters.

We started Apex to fix that. The same xG math, the same Monte Carlo rigour, the same transparency — but built for the leagues African punters actually bet on. NPFL. AFCON. CAF. And the rest.

What we believe

Four values, every commit.

01

Show the math

Every True Odds, every EV, every probability is traceable. No black boxes.

02

Local first

African leagues, African punters, Naira pricing. The market we serve is the market we live in.

03

Play responsibly

We sell probability, not encouragement. We refuse to flag low-confidence markets as VALUE.

04

Ship the truth

If the model says 0.55, we say 0.55 — not 0.6, not 0.7. Honest numbers build trust over years, not weeks.
