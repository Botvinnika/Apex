Privacy policy

What personal data we collect, why, and how to have it removed.

Template notice. A good-faith starting point, not legal advice. Have a qualified Nigerian data-protection practitioner review this — and confirm your NDPR filing obligations — before relying on it.

1. Who is responsible

ApexSports Analytics, Lagos, Nigeria, is the data controller for the personal data described here. Data protection enquiries: privacy@apexsports.ng.

2. What we collect

  • Account data — name, email address, and a hashed password.
  • Billing data — plan, billing history, and the last four digits and expiry of your card. Full card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never reach our servers.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, features used, and approximate region derived from IP address, used to operate and improve the service.
  • Technical data — browser type, device type, and IP address, held in server logs for security and diagnostics.
  • Correspondence — messages you send us and our replies.

We do not collect special-category data, and we ask you not to send it to us.

3. Why we process it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service — performance of our contract with you.
  • To take payment — performance of contract, and legal obligation for tax and accounting records.
  • To keep the service secure — our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and unauthorised access.
  • To improve the product — legitimate interest in understanding which features are used, using aggregated data wherever possible.
  • To send the research digest — your consent, withdrawable at any time via the unsubscribe link in every email.

4. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for sign-in and security. Your theme preference is kept in your browser's local storage, not sent to us. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell or share data with ad networks.

5. Who we share it with

Only with processors necessary to run the service — hosting, payment processing, email delivery, and error monitoring — each bound by contract to process data only on our instructions. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data where required by law or to establish or defend legal claims.

Some processors operate outside Nigeria. Where data is transferred abroad, we rely on contractual safeguards requiring a comparable standard of protection, consistent with NDPR requirements.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data — while your account is active, then 12 months.
  • Billing records — 6 years, to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Server logs — 90 days.
  • Marketing consent records — until you withdraw consent, plus 12 months as proof the consent existed.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPR you may:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Have inaccurate data corrected;
  • Request deletion where we have no continuing lawful basis to keep it;
  • Object to, or request restriction of, processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing;
  • Complain to the supervisory authority if you believe we have handled your data unlawfully.

Write to privacy@apexsports.ng. We respond within 30 days and will not charge you for a reasonable request.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are stored using a modern one-way hashing algorithm, and administrative access is restricted and logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

9. Children

The service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be notified by email or prominent site notice before taking effect. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
