Model evolution

From a Python notebook to a production engine.

Six major model versions, all benchmarked, all kept on disk.

v1
2023 · NPFL only

Dixon-Coles baseline

Poisson attack/defence. 12 NPFL teams. Open-sourced on GitHub. 63% accuracy on full-time result.

v2
2024 · Multi-league

xG + form + travel

Expected Goals layer. Form over last 10. Travel distance. 31 leagues. 71% accuracy.

v3
2024 · Q4

Monte Carlo at scale

10,000 simulations per match. EV in ₦ per ₦1,000 shipped. Trap detection. 76% accuracy.

v3.5
2025 · Q4

Public Ledger launch

Every prediction timestamped and visible. Dataset cards published. Calibration drift detection added.

v4
2026 · Now

147 leagues · 4 sports

Football, basketball, tennis, baseball. Weather, lineups, congestion. 94.7% value-pick hit rate.

The math

Five equations, one engine.

If you can read Python and you're not scared of LaTeX, this is the whole model.

1. Expected Goals (per shot)

A logistic regression over location, angle, assist type, and pressure. Trained on 4.1M historical shots since 2017.

xG = σ(w₀ + w₁·loc + w₂·angle + w₃·assist + w₄·pressure + w₅·body)

2. Match outcome probability

Dixon-Coles adjusted Poisson over team-level attack and defence strengths.

P(home = x, away = y) = λ_h^x · e^(-λ_h) / x! · μ_a^y · e^(-μ_a) / y! · φ(λ, μ, ρ, x, y)

3. Monte Carlo simulation

For each match, we sample 10,000+ game states from the joint distribution and tally outcomes.

TrueOdds(market) = 1 / [ (1/N) · Σ 1[market(sᵢ) holds] ]

4. Expected Value

Per ₦1,000 staked. Positive EV = the model thinks the market is mispriced.

EV = (TrueProb · Odds − 1) · Stake

5. Kelly stake

Optimal bet size as a fraction of bankroll. We never recommend more than 5% of bankroll on a single leg.

f* = (TrueProb · Odds − 1) / (Odds − 1) · min(1, 0.05)
Honest disclosures

Where the model is wrong.

Every model has blind spots. Here are ours.

Bias · known

Long-tail leagues

Lower-tier leagues (NPFL second division, regional Kenyan cups) have less data — the model's confidence drops below 50% accuracy there. We still ship the data, but flag it.

Refuses

Coin-flip matches

When TrueProb sits between 30% and 70%, we mark the market as FAIR and refuse to flag it. We'd rather miss a pick than lie about edge.

Avoids

Same-match correlation

Two legs from the same match are highly correlated. The Strategy Builder blocks them or warns loudly. The math would not catch this otherwise.
